Privacy Policy

Intelligence Cube · DirimSoft · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Android app Intelligence Cube (package name com.intelligencecube.game), developed by DirimSoft, handles information.

Information we collect

Intelligence Cube does not collect, process, store or share any personal data.

  • The app does not connect to the internet and does not request the internet permission.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels) and scores are saved only on your device so that you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if advertising is added in a future version), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at dirimsoftware@gmail.com.
