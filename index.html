How to play

Simple rules, tricky paths.

  1. Roll the cube with the four arrows. Each roll moves it one tile.
  2. Tiles fall behind you. You can never step on the same tile twice.
  3. Clear every tile before the time runs out. Stuck? Tap R and try a new route.

Privacy
